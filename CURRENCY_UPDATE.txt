SaveSales Currency Update

Store currencies included:
BRL, USD, CAD, GBP, AUD, NZD, EUR, CHF, SEK, NOK, DKK, PLN, SGD, ZAR, INR

Behavior:
- Currency is an app-wide, LAN-synchronized setting.
- Changing currency requires the admin password.
- Existing numeric catalog prices are NOT exchange-rate converted when the currency changes.
- New sales store the active currency permanently.
- Historical sales/receipts retain their original currency.
- Mixed-currency Daily Tracking and HTML statements show totals separately instead of adding unlike currencies.
- Existing historical sales created before this update are treated as BRL for backward compatibility.

Test:
  py -3.12 main.py

Then rebuild SaveSales.exe after verifying the feature.
